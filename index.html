What we build

Focused tools for real business problems.

Every project starts with a specific problem. These are the kinds of solutions we build most often, with an example of each.

Opportunity discovery agents

AI agents that search public sources for requests and signals that match your criteria, and check each one against your rules before it reaches you.

For exampleSidepipe, our own product, finds and qualifies business opportunities and shows the source behind every match.

Research and summaries

Gather information about companies, markets or requests and turn it into short, structured summaries that separate confirmed facts from educated guesses.

For exampleBackground research on a prospect, with each fact marked as verified, inferred or unknown.

Document processing

Pull tables and key fields out of PDFs, spreadsheets and structured files, so the data lands where your team actually uses it.

For exampleTurning PDF bills of materials into structured data that updates a shared project tracker.

Workflow automation and integrations

Connect the tools you already use and automate the steps between them: scheduled jobs, approvals, notifications and data sync.

For exampleAutomatic updates to a Smartsheet project plan, scheduled billing emails and online card payments through Stripe.

Custom apps, dashboards and websites

Web applications that work on any phone, with sign-in, roles and permissions, dashboards and reports, designed around the way you work.

For examplePulse and Cadence, our scheduling and time & attendance apps, are built exactly this way.

Something else on your mind?

If it involves repetitive work, or information you have but can’t easily use, it’s worth a conversation.

How we work

Small steps. Useful results.

No long specifications and no big-bang launches. We get something useful into your hands early, then make it better with you.

  1. 01

    Understand the problem

    We start with how the work happens today: who does it, how long it takes and what a good result would look like.

    Clarity before code.
  2. 02

    Build a focused solution

    We build the smallest thing that solves the real problem, connecting to the tools you already use wherever we can.

    Useful early, not perfect later.
  3. 03

    Test and improve

    You use it on real work. We look at what changed, fix what gets in the way and improve it from there.

    Better with every round.
