About Cashew

Practical software, built to be used.

Cashew Solutions, OPC is a software and AI solutions business. We build and run our own products, and we build focused tools for businesses that want to spend less time on routine work and more time on customers and growth.

What we believe

Technology should earn its place. We would rather solve one real problem well than sell a long list of features, and we’re upfront about what software and AI can and can’t do for your situation.

AI is useful when it saves time on searching, reading, sorting and drafting, with a person deciding what happens next. That is how we design our own products, and how we approach client work.

What we make

  • Sidepipe finds business opportunities that match your criteria and shows the evidence behind each one.
  • Pulse gives multi-department teams one live schedule in place of spreadsheets.
  • Cadence handles time & attendance, approvals and payroll runs for hourly teams.

We also build custom applications, automations and document workflows for specific business needs. See examples of our work.
